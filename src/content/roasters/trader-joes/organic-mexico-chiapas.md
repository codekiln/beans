## location: Chiapas

Chiapas is Mexico's southernmost state, bordering Guatemala, and its highlands
supply most of the country's exported arabica. Coffee there is largely grown by
smallholders in cooperatives, often under shade at moderate elevation, which is
part of why Chiapas lots show up so often with organic certification.

Grocery-shelf Chiapas coffee at a medium roast tends toward the gentle end of the
Mexican profile: cocoa, nut, and soft acidity rather than the bright fruit of a
washed Kenya. Worth noting against the light-roast Kenyas that dominate the rest
of this log.
