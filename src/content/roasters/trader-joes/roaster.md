- US grocery chain selling coffee under its own private label rather than roasting in house.
- Individual bags name the origin and roast level but not the contract roaster, so lot-level traceability is thinner than with a named specialty roaster.
- Carries a rotating mix of single-origin and blended coffees, with several organic and fair-trade options in the standing lineup.
- Website: [traderjoes.com](https://www.traderjoes.com/)
