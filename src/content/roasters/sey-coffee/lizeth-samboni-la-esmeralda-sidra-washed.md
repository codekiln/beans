varietals. SEY believes supporting these efforts plays a meaningful role in the
increased quality it has seen across Huila in recent years.

## about Sidra

While its exact germ line is unknown, the variety colloquially known as Sidra in
Ecuador, and now cultivated in many coffee-growing countries around the world,
is understood to be an Ethiopian Landrace. Ethiopia is widely acknowledged as
the geographic origin of coffee and is home to extraordinary genetic diversity,
with DNA testing identifying more than 60 distinct varieties. How these
varieties made their way from Ethiopia to other parts of the world remains the
subject of ongoing research and debate. As climate change makes coffee
increasingly difficult to cultivate, Ethiopian Landrace germ lines are also
playing an important role in the development of new varieties and hybrids.
