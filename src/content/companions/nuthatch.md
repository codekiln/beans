kettle on the stump.  
nuthatch halfway down the pine,  
feet still pointing up.  
checks a crack in the bark  
while i rinse the filter.  

blue-gray back,  
warm rust under the wing.  
a seed tucked away  
for later.  

by the time the cup cools  
has worked around  
the other side of the trunk.  
a small call from there.
