## reflection

peel the gilded  
berry of guilt.  
go grind gratitude,  
candy its density,  
see the autumn  
transform into wind.  

sweet round hips  
of sour round sun  
peeking into books'  
ancient sheafs,  
dissecting the  
seed body anatomy,  
flavor heredity.  

over her mountain  
shoulder glance,  
the shade in  
bird song weaves  
tesseractional  
riposte to  
the grinder's  
dark maw.  

the whole embrace  
whiffs of generosity,  
while fastens are  
unhooked, the milk  
pours into the forest  
in an inexorable flow  
white rivers  
seeking dark  
cavern comfort.
