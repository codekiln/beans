## thinking: travelogue app

### basic idea

make it as easy and fun as possible  
to reflect on, create and share  
memories and experiences that happened  
in specific places,  
presentational format vs archival.  

### "back of house"

upload / import  
record reflections  
(write / voice)  
curate / edit  
return to a  
scrapbook aesthetic  

### "front of house"

share  
"comment"  
add cards for  
bulletin board  
and yarn  

### design / aesthetic

simplify complex images  
and map elements  
with an illustrative overlay  
that gradually fades  
to the real photo / map  
with each tap or scroll  

illustrative styles are  
themed with prompt collections  
by default; they  
increase signal to  
noise by starting  
simple (3 lines)  
then becoming more  
detailed  

### trip representations

1. timelines  
   (like subway maps)  
   scroll; heading; focus; paragraph about  
   what happened; fade in  
2. geographical  
   maps  
3. "hand-drawn"  
   diagrams / illustrations
